5The annotation layer

every candidate cardthe note under the card: what the candidate's number cannot know. Off by default; the card itself carries the value, the source and the date.
the lanes cardthe note under the chart: a heart rate and a pace are two kinds of number, and neither is turned into the other.
the field-test cardthe note under the card: what the physiological-cost curve carries, and that other rules are published for a field test.
the header cardthe note under the card: what a no-value-with-a-reason means, and what the choice is tied to.
the history cardthe note under the list: what each row is, and what the app does not say about it.
the used framethe note under each card: where the ladder's rung comes from, what the base's bands read, what the session keeps, and which number the ledger and the load answer against.
this pagethe section notes, the frame captions, and the list of what the prototype does not do — all here, under the toggle.
What this prototype does not do
It does not rank the candidates, mark one as the one to take, warn that a candidate is old, show a score, or colour anything as a problem. It does not merge the watch's zone model with the athlete's threshold, and it does not re-band the ledger.
