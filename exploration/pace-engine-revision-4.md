Parent: one correction from the spine, for the ladder's LT rung.

**The book's LT anchor is a band, not a point.** "Your race pace for **15K or
10 miles**" (elites: 15K-to-HM) — a "15K pace" alone is the **fast edge** of
that band. The truer statement of the rule is **"the pace you could race for
about an hour"**; the 15K / 10-mile route is the book's *approximation* of it.
The spine also tabulates **five ways to find LT pace** (lactate test; a
45–60-minute race; a 45-minute time trial; a training-software estimate; HR
at 82–90 % maxHR / 76–87 % HRR) — `exploration/pfitzinger-spine.md` §5.5.1.

So please:

1. **The LT rung carries a band of paces, not a single number** — drawn as a
   band on the pace axis (the fast edge and the slow edge), with the rule
   named. Where your engine computes a single "15K pace", that is the fast
   edge; say so in the note rather than presenting it as the whole rule.
2. **The caption states the rule as the book states it** — "the pace you
   could race for about an hour" — with the 15K/10-mile route marked as the
   book's approximation, and with the five ways listed in the note (the
   threshold-setup thread is designing the athlete-facing version of them).
3. If a **45-minute time trial** is the book's own threshold-pace test, the
   ladder's source line may name it as a route to the LT band — but the
   ladder stays about paces and bands; the test itself belongs to the
   threshold setup.
