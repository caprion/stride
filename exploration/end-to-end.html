Stride — the whole product, one athlete

One synthetic athlete, one window (7 Sep – 24 Nov 2026, 58 runs), every destination and every contextual surface, in the order a runner meets them. Every number is read from exploration/athlete.js — the same file a later thread will build the Beta's five screens from. The designs are not re-derived here: the ledger, the ladder, the curve, the threshold and the eleven cards are assembled from the prototypes that already exist.
